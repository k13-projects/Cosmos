## Test-harness bugs I found and fixed in my own scripts (not product bugs)
Documenting these because they produced real-looking false failures mid-run, and the same class
of mistake will bite the next person who scripts this wheel:
1. **A modal dialog panel is routinely taller than the viewport** (that's the whole point of the
   fix) — aiming `mouse.wheel` at the dialog element's own bounding-box center can land far
   off-screen. Fix: aim at the scroll container's box instead (`div[data-lenis-prevent]`, always
   `fixed inset-0`, i.e. exactly the viewport).
2. **`[data-spoke]:not([aria-hidden]).first` is not reliably on-screen.** The wheel repeats its
   six plates 24 times around the full 360deg ring; only the six "primary" copies are
   interactive, and at any instant most of those six sit on the invisible far side of the circle.
   Fix: query `getBoundingClientRect()` for all candidates and pick one actually inside the
   viewport and clear of the fixed header band.
3. **Playwright's `locator.is_visible()` does not check an ancestor's opacity or clipped
   max-height.** It reported the CLOSED mobile-nav sheet's duplicate "Order online" button as
   visible (it has a non-empty own bounding box and no `visibility:hidden`), even though the
   sheet ancestor is `opacity-0` + `max-h-0` when closed. Fix: walk ancestors checking computed
   `opacity` and `maxHeight` before trusting `is_visible()`.

None of these were product defects — confirmed by finding the correctly-targeted element and
re-measuring clean in every case (see the `qa.json` sidecar for before/after numbers on each).
