## Risks
- **QA-06, two photo bands upscale at 1920 and it is a build-script bug, not a photo bug.**
  `scripts/build-assets.sh` uses `sips -Z 2048`, which constrains the longest edge; both sources are
  portrait, so that starves the width these full-bleed bands actually need. band-chicken loses real
  resolution (1366 delivered when 1690 is available) and band-phone is upscaled by the pipeline
  itself (1639 from a 1510 native), costing bytes for no detail. Natalia's call.
- **Horizon is still unlicensed.** My QA-03 fix sizes the legal page title against the real strings

## Evolution
Two of my own measurements came back clean and were wrong, and both were the same failure: a check
that never actually ran. A tight `scrollTo` then measure loop reads pre-scroll positions when Lenis
is virtualizing scroll, and reading `scroll-margin-top` on a section says nothing when the offset
lives in `scroll-padding-top` on `html`. The habit worth keeping is that a clean result on a
check I have not seen fail once is itself suspicious: prove the mechanism moved before trusting
what it reports. Both are written up in the report rather than quietly deleted.
