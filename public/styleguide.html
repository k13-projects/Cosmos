The one honest failure. White body copy on brand magenta #CD1AE0 measures 4.36:1 and misses AA (4.5:1) at 15–17 px. The values band is the only place this occurs. Typography

Two faces. Horizon (Adobe Fonts) is the brand display face from the guide: wide, heavy, geometric, always uppercase. Poppins carries everything else. Bookmania is listed in the guide but appears nowhere in the mockup, so it is not part of the web system.

Display — Horizon, stand-in Archivo Expanded Black

every H1/H2

Horizon is not free. Until THG confirms an Adobe Fonts seat, the display face is Archivo at its widest width axis (font-stretch:125%) and weight 900 — the closest free wide-geometric grotesque. The swap is isolated in one token, --font-display, so it is a one-line change later.

Behaviour

Motion

House K13 motion DNA, tuned to a loud brand: content rises in, yellow surfaces catch a light sweep, plates breathe. Three easings, nothing else. Every demo on this page loops so you can see it; on the site each of these fires once on scroll into view. All of it collapses under prefers-reduced-motion.

Interaction timing and reduced motion

global

Hover, focus and colour changes are always 0.3 s on the UI easing. Reveals are 0.8 s on the brand easing. Overshoot is reserved for badges and stamps and is used sparingly.

@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) { * { animation: none !important; transition: none !important } }
--ease-brand
cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1) — reveals, wipes, slides
--ease-ui
cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1) — 0.3 s hover/focus
--ease-pop
cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1) — badges, "coming soon" stamps
Reduced motion
All animation and transition off; the wipe's clip-path is cleared, the sheen layer is removed, hover lifts are neutralised. Nothing is hidden, everything lands in its end state.
Scroll
Lenis smooth scroll is disabled entirely under reduced motion.

Shapes, rhythm and voice

Cosmos has one shape language: the pill and the big-radius rectangle. Nothing on this site has a sharp corner except a full-bleed photo band.

Voice

all copy

Casual, warm, bold. The brand guide's own sliders put Cosmos at casual, warm, progressive, accessible, simple. The line to hold in your head is Lorena's own: good food, good vibes, big flavor.

DoUse Lorena's copy verbatim. Short sentences. Second person. Food words over marketing words. Say plainly when something is not ready yet.
Don'tNo em or en dashes anywhere visitor-facing, rewrite around them with commas. No invented menu items, prices, hours or reviews. No exclamation stacking. No "artisanal", "curated", "elevated".
Source of truth
All strings live in lib/content.ts with provenance comments. Components never hardcode copy.
Client phrasing
"food near me" reads as SEO but it is the client's own line: keep it.
Spelling
"Flavour" in the values band and "FLAVOR" in the headline are both hers. Kept as written, raised with her.
Honesty
Missing ordering links, missing prices and missing contact details are shown as plain "coming soon" states, never as dead buttons or invented data.
